The Android Mobile Email Client Mozilla Thunderbird does not support OpenPGP directly. Uses OpenKeychain to decrypt messages and optionally encrypt messages. Available in the Google Play Store or for F-Droid.

### Key Facts

* Developer/Publisher: [Mozilla](https://foundation.mozilla.org/)
* License: Open Source (MPL)
* Price: Free ([Donations](https://give.thunderbird.net))
* Web: [Get Thunderbird Mobile](https://www.thunderbird.net/mobile)
* FAQ: [OpenPGP in Thunderbird - HOWTO](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/openpgp-thunderbird-android-howto)
